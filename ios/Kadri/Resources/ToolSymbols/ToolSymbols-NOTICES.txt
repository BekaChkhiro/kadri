Coverage
Exact-family means the illustration depicts that family, not a certified model or size. Family means a generic family silhouette paired with a specific text label/profile. Placeholder means a generic toolbox symbol because no suitable exact depiction was verified in the selected library; it must not be described as an accurate illustration of that tool.
